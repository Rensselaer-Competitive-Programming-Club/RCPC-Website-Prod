We are the Rensselaer Competitive Programming Club!

Join a community of problem solvers! Whether you want to sharpen your coding skills, strengthen your problem-solving mindset, or prepare for technical interviews, RCPC is here to help!

Join Us! Learn More

Why join RCPC?

  • Build strong foundations in algorithms and data structures
  • Gain hands-on practice with coding challenges and competitions
  • Improve your problem-solving speed and accuracy
  • Meet peers who share your interest in programming and logic

We meet weekly on Wednesdays, 6-8 PM in Sage 4101!

Looking Ahead

RCPC is a brand-new club at RPI. Our goal is to grow into a team that represents Rensselaer in regional and international programming contests, while building a supportive space for students to challenge themselves and learn together.
